# Security policy

We take the security of Wemember and of the data our customers entrust to it seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **do not** open a public issue for security problems.

Report it privately through GitHub: open the repository concerned, go to the **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**. This creates a private advisory that only the Wemember maintainers can see.

Please include:

- the affected repository, component or URL;
- steps to reproduce, and what an attacker could achieve;
- any proof of concept, logs or screenshots (with secrets and personal data removed).

We will acknowledge your report, keep you informed while we investigate, and credit you in the advisory if you wish.

## Scope

- The Wemember service at `app.wemember.ai`, including its MCP endpoint `https://app.wemember.ai/mcp`.
- The repositories in the [wemember-ai](https://github.com/wemember-ai) organization.

Please do not access or modify data that is not yours, degrade the service for others, or use social engineering. Test only with accounts and workspaces you own.
